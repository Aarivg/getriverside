# Artwork sources

The campus background and the Dominion, Loudoun County, Heritage, Stone Bridge,
Tuscarora, and Potomac Falls tile images came from the
[archived GetRiverside site](https://web.archive.org/web/20250301171057/https://getriverside.com/).

The remaining school logos were obtained from school pages in the Loudoun County
Public Schools system:

- [Freedom High School](https://www.lcps.org/o/fhs)
- [Woodgrove High School](https://www.lcps.org/o/whs)
- [Independence High School](https://www.lcps.org/o/ihs)
- [John Champe High School](https://www.lcps.org/o/jch)
- [Riverside High School](https://www.lcps.org/o/rvh)

School names and logos belong to their respective schools. This repository is
an unofficial fan recreation and is not affiliated with Loudoun County Public
Schools.
